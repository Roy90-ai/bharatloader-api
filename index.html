A few things worth knowing.

How do I download a video?

Copy a public video link, use Paste or type it into the field, then select Download Video. Confirm the checkbox and open the returned stream. Use your browser’s save option when available.

Why can an Instagram link fail?

Instagram may restrict access based on authentication, the account, or the network. Public links can also fail. Try another accessible link; extraction cannot guarantee access to every video.

Are these videos always HD or watermark-free?

Available quality, audio, and watermarks depend on the stream returned by the platform. This app does not remove embedded logos or merge separate audio tracks.

Is the verification checkbox bot protection?

It is a manual confirmation step with a short preparation delay. It does not provide CAPTCHA verification or independent security protection.

PRIVACY POLICY · DRAFT

Your links. Your privacy.

BharatLoader does not maintain a database of pasted links, downloaded clips, or extracted media metadata. It does not intentionally cache media files or maintain a personal viewing-history profile. Links are processed to resolve streams, and platform requests are made by the server.

Clipboard access happens only when you press Paste. Only the submitted link is sent to the local backend. Platform and thumbnail servers receive requests needed to provide the video preview and stream.

Runtime and extractor error logs can contain URLs and technical metadata. This app therefore does not promise that no link or metadata ever appears in logs. Hosting and third-party logging policies must be reviewed before public launch.
